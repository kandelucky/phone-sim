Hello

A demo app for the phone simulator.
Each button opens one thing to try: touch, vibration, links, ads.
Every action writes a line in the console.

Touch

1.0×

At 1× swipe the picture any way. Two fingers apart to zoom in (in the simulator: the mouse wheel over the picture); zoomed in, one finger moves it. A double tap goes back to 1×.

Vibrate

Each button buzzes the phone. In the simulator a zigzag lights up on each side of the phone for every buzz, and the console writes the pattern.

Links

Each link leaves the app, as on a phone: the app goes to the background and the simulator shows the app that would open. ◁ brings you back. WhatsApp opens only if it is installed: the console says so.

Ads

A banner and a full-screen ad through the AdMob plugin; in the simulator they are stand-ins. ⚙ → Ads → No ads makes every ad fail, as offline: the app must go on.
